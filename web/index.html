    Required columns: pao2,fio2. Optional: paco2,peep,map,age,spo2,patm,patient_id. All files are processed in your browser; no upload to a server.

    Clinical scope: The Berlin definition requires onset within one week, bilateral opacities, edema not fully explained by cardiac failure or fluid overload, and appropriate ventilatory pressure. This tool calculates oxygenation ranges and does not diagnose ARDS or recommend treatment. The later Global Definition (2024) expands supported contexts; this app intentionally implements Berlin (2012) ranges only.

    References: Berlin Definition (JAMA, 2012). No external scripts, storage, accounts, tracking, or automatic network submissions. Avoid patient-identifying data on shared computers. For research and education; not a medical device.
